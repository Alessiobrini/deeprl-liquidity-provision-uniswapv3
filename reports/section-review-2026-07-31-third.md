# Section-by-section review, third pass (2026-07-31)

Method: we reviewed `paper/RL_for_liquidity_provisioning/icaif_main.tex` section by section in reading order. For each section we ran `review-brini` (line level: voice, acronyms, captions, active voice, figures, citations) and `review-zeropaper` (document level: prose economy, consistency, referee, defensibility, claim grounding, page budget) in parallel, then an adversarial critic verified every proposal against the source and rejected on doubt. Each section handed its canonical terms, symbols, numbers, and open threads forward to the next, so continuity defects that only appear when the paper is read top to bottom are caught here. This report is find-only. Nothing has been applied.

## Executive summary

We confirmed **116 findings** across seven sections, from **203 raw proposals** (39 explicitly rejected, the remainder folded into merged entries). Severity split: **0 high, 45 medium, 71 low**. No finding requires a number to change, and the net page effect of the confirmed set is a cut.

The eight most consequential items, most severe first.

1. **The headline collapse result is stated in four different scopes and two different objects** (Abstract L85, Introduction L122, Method L170, Results L276). The Results give the narrowest and best supported form, one distinct observed action per pool on the first seed. Three other sites state it wider, and two of them say "width" although the action set includes label 0, which holds rather than executes a width.
2. **The same collapse result is reported on one seed with no defense anywhere** (Results L276, Limitations L361). Every other learned result averages the two seeds declared at L166 and L197. This is the first objection a referee will raise against the paper's central negative finding.
3. **A prespecified contrast is never reported** (Method L177, Results L281 and L303). Four within-pool contrasts are declared, three are reported, and the four-member Holm family size is quoted twice while the oracle against the decision stump appears nowhere.
4. **The gated follow-up run is described differently in method and results** (L179 prespecifies reward-only normalization, L305 reports reward-only normalization and validation-selected entropy). A treatment the Results attribute to the run is absent from its prespecification, which undercuts the framing of the run as prespecified.
5. **Figure 2's caption points the reader against the paper's own conclusion** (L315). The panels carry no significance marking, unlike Figure 1, and "Blue favors recentering" reads the mostly blue high-tier panels as evidence for recentering while L319 reports that no favorable cell survives correction.
6. **The two tick spacings appear nowhere in the paper** (L164, L225, gloss owed since L124). A reader cannot map a label onto a tick count, cannot reconstruct the matched grid of 480, 540, and 600 raw ticks, and cannot check either against the reported price ratios.
7. **Two symbol collisions inside the method section** (L146 against L172, L155 against L170). $H$ carries both the value of the held basket, the anchor of the reward, and the evaluation horizon in hours. $s$ carries both the swap index and the decision state, nine and fifteen lines apart respectively.
8. **The paper's most repeated claim points at an exhibit that cannot show it** (L234). Figure 1 plots matched-minus-nominal effects with intervals and carries no ranking information, so the four-of-six reordering claim is reconstructible only by combining Figure 1 with Table 2, which arrives in the next subsection.

## Cross-section flow threads

Reviewing in order surfaced six document-level threads that no single-section pass would have found.

**1. One result, four scopes and two objects (the single-action thread).** This is the paper's central negative finding and it is the least stable text in the draft. The Abstract says "the learned policies, evaluated deterministically, select only one observed action per pool", the Introduction at L122 says "every learned arm turns out to execute a single width", the method at L170 says "the learned deterministic policies execute a single width per pool", and the Results at L276 say "every learned algorithm's first-seed deterministic policy uses one distinct observed action in every pool" and then immediately revert to "a single constant width". The Results wording is the narrowest and the only one the evidence supports. It should be settled once and propagated to all four sites, with "action" replacing "width" everywhere, because label 0 is defined at L164 as holding. The Conclusion never states the result at all, which the authors may want to reconsider once a line frees up.

**2. Prespecification integrity.** The paper's stated contribution is a prespecified protocol, and three seams in that protocol are open. The four contrasts declared at L177 yield three reported ones. The gated follow-up's treatment differs between L179 and L305, and its Holm family is never stated, which matters because L305 reports an adjusted $p=.0001$ against a resolution floor asserted at L281 that only a one-member family reaches. The three sensitivity variants reported at L328 (reward shaping against a shadow position, an LVR reward, and input-layer state normalization) are declared nowhere in Sec. 3. Each of these is cheap to close in one clause and expensive to leave for a referee.

**3. Reconstructibility and provenance.** A reader cannot rebuild the experiment from the text. The two tick spacings are absent, so the action-set half-widths and the matched grid cannot be recovered. The training discount factor appears only inside the wrapper parenthetical of the gated follow-up, so the discount used for the five primary learned arms is never stated. The hourly grid's fill rule for swapless hours is unstated although it feeds the fee attribution and the state variables. The decision schedule for the fixed-width, volatility-proportional, and IL-minimizing heuristics is unstated although the number of mints sets the gas charge and the schedule result at L328 leans on it. ILMinimizer and the volatility-proportional arm are given one identical mechanism although Table 2 separates them in every pool. No implementation library is named anywhere, so "RecurrentPPO" and "VecNormalize" are unattributed and four of the five learned arms carry no citation. Holm and the moving-block bootstrap, which produce every interval and p-value in the paper, carry no methodological citation.

**4. The terminology ledger and where it drifts.** The paper fixes a good canonical vocabulary early and then leaks it at a small number of predictable sites. The per-pool leader is "highest-mean strategy" (L124, L234, L272, L346, L368) but becomes "pool winners" at L353 and L366, so this fix has two sites and "pool winner" should survive only in the Table 2 caption's bolding convention. The diagnostic object is "state-contingent headroom" but becomes "state-contingent opportunity" at L118 and L355 and "ex-post headroom" at L368, again two or three sites. The headline negative result has five renderings (L118, L242, L272, L344, L368), of which "every mean sits below holding" should be the one. The point-estimate verdict is "descriptively favorable" but becomes "descriptively better" at L319, in the very section that earns the term. The cost variant is "the conversion treatment" but becomes "conversion friction" at L319. The fee split is low-tier and high-tier everywhere except the Abstract and the axes table at L347, one row below a canonical use. The action object is "action set" except at L134, where an unqualified "action space" collides with it. The volatility arm is abbreviated two ways across Table 2 and the Figure 1 axis. None of these costs a line to fix, and each is exactly what a careful referee marks.

**5. Forward references and exhibit order.** Three sites break reading order. The Results open at L217 with a pointer to `tab:axes`, which is declared at L334 in the Discussion and numbers as Table 4, before Tables 2 and 3 have been referenced, and the same target is pointed at again at L353. Subsec. 4.1 leans on Table 2 twice, once for the nominal level of $-\$335$ and once for the four-of-six reordering claim, before Subsec. 4.2 introduces the table. The method at L170 leans on a Results finding as already established. Deleting the L217 pointer restores first-reference order and gives the Discussion sole ownership of Table 4.

**6. Story arc and repetition budget.** The four-axes spine is sound and the sections hand off cleanly, but the claim that CLEB controls all four axes at once is asserted five times (L85, L118, L127, L134, L368) and the reusable-protocol claim three times (L118, L127, L368) while it is developed properly only once, at L210. The Conclusion states its own geometry result twice inside three sentences. Deleting the weaker copy at each site is the main source of the page budget this pass needs, and the arithmetic works out: the confirmed cuts in the Introduction, Related Work, Method, Results, Discussion, and Conclusion exceed the confirmed additions (the schedule clause, the seed clause, the tick spacings, the fourth-contrast clause, the Holm-family clause, the cost-blind caveat, and the Figure 2 caption sentence), so the paper ends shorter than it starts.
